What it's all about

Around Armstrong is a weekly show about the people, clubs, businesses and events in Armstrong and Spallumcheen. Every Friday you get what's on around town, a guest from the community, and a few shout-outs and milestones worth celebrating.

Watch it over the weekend on Facebook, YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, or right here if you'd rather skip social media.

I can't catch everything that happens around here. For the full list of events, the Armstrong Spallumcheen Chamber of Commerce has a great calendar, and the clubs, the City and the Township are good about posting their own updates.

Every Friday

  1. Around town. What's on this week, from events to Parks and Rec to the library.
  2. Community guest. Someone from around town pulls up a chair to talk about what they're up to.
  3. Milestones. Big birthdays, anniversaries, and local businesses hitting a big number.
  4. Community shout-out. A thank-you or a fun nod to someone around town.
