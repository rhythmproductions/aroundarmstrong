What the show is

Local stories, events coming up, news from the clubs and non-profits around here, what's new with local businesses, and a sit-down with somebody from around town.

New episode every Friday, so you can catch up over the weekend and keep up with what's going on around here.

Made in Armstrong by someone who lives here.

Every Friday

  1. Around town. Local stories from Armstrong and area.
  2. Local business. What's new with the people running things around here.
  3. Community corner. Events this weekend, plus news from the clubs and non-profits.
  4. The sit-down. One guest from around town, in the studio or on location.

Sponsor the show

The people who watch Around Armstrong are the people who live here. If your business wants to be in front of them every week, there are a few ways in, sold by the month rather than by the episode.

  • Title sponsor. One only. Mentioned in the open and the sign-off, logo on the thumbnail and end card, a 20 to 30 second read every episode.
  • Segment sponsor. Up to three. One segment "presented by" you, logo on screen.
  • Local supporter. Your logo on the end card every week, with a thank-you.
  • Single feature. One episode, no commitment.

Email hello@aroundarmstrong.ca and I'll send the one-page sheet with the numbers.

Got something happening?

An event, a new business opening, a fundraiser, or a story you think the town should hear about. Send it to hello@aroundarmstrong.ca. I read everything, and if it fits the week, it's in.
