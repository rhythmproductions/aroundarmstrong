What it's all about

Around Armstrong is a weekly show about the people, clubs, businesses and events in Armstrong and Spallumcheen. Every Friday we talk about upcoming events, sit down with guests from the community, and share a few shout-outs and milestones we're celebrating.

Filmed right here in Armstrong at the Rhythm Productions studio.

You can watch episodes on Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or X, or right here on this website if you'd rather skip social media.

There's so much going on in our community, it's hard to catch everything. Another great resource is the Armstrong Spallumcheen Chamber of Commerce, which has a great calendar of events coming up, and the clubs, the City and the Township are good about posting their own updates.

Every Friday

  1. Around town. What's on this week, from events to Parks and Rec to the library.
  2. Community guests. People from around town pull up a chair and talk about what they're up to.
  3. Milestones. Big birthdays, anniversaries, and local businesses hitting a big number.
  4. Community shout-out. A thank-you or a fun nod to someone around town.

How to get on the show

Around Armstrong is 100% filmed in studio, right here in Armstrong at the Rhythm Productions studio.

If you've got something going on, I'd love to hear about it. There are two ways to get on the show:

A mention. Send the details and a poster or a couple of photos at least two weeks ahead, and I'll get it on the show. Events, milestones, shout-outs, club updates. Free for everyone.

A guest spot. Local non-profits and community groups can come into the studio for a few minutes and tell the town about your group and any events coming up. Spots are limited each week, so the earlier you ask, the sooner we can get you on the show. Also free.
