What the show is

Around Armstrong is a weekly video show covering Armstrong, BC. Every Friday: what council decided Monday night, what's happening with roadwork and the schools, one local business and what's new with them, what's on this weekend, and a sit-down with somebody from around town.

Armstrong and Spallumcheen mostly, and the odd story from Enderby to Vernon when it matters here.

Made in Armstrong by someone who lives here. No opinions on the contentious stuff and no call-ins. What happened this week, so you can catch up over the weekend.

Every Friday

  1. The week in Armstrong. What council decided Monday night, roadwork, the schools, whatever else happened.
  2. Business spotlight. One local business and what's new with them.
  3. Community corner. What's on this weekend, from the farmers' market to whatever's at the arena.
  4. The interview. One guest, in the studio or on location.

Sponsor the show

The people who watch Around Armstrong are the people who live here. If your business wants to be in front of them every week, there are a few ways in, sold by the month rather than by the episode.

  • Title sponsor. One only. Mentioned in the open and the sign-off, logo on the thumbnail and end card, a 20 to 30 second read every episode.
  • Segment sponsor. Up to three. One segment "presented by" you, logo on screen.
  • Local supporter. Your logo on the end card every week, with a thank-you.
  • Single feature. One episode, no commitment.

An event, a new business opening, a fundraiser, or a story you think the town should hear about. Send it to hello@aroundarmstrong.ca. I read everything, and if it fits the week, it's in.
